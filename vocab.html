Target Academic Word List (Passages 1 & 2)

Mastering these 15 key academic terms from the workshop passages will significantly boost your paraphrase recognition in Band 6 Reading items.

Operational autonomy Freedom of workers to control their tasks.
Proprietary algorithm Privately owned, non-public computer code.
Task restructuring Altering job duties without firing staff.
Biometric telemetry Automated biological/physical data tracking.
Exception-handling Humans stepping in when software limits fail.
Qualitative feedback Descriptive, text-based opinion data.
Prohibitive costs Expenses too high to afford.
Skill mismatch Gap between worker skills & job needs.
